# Overall Impression

The best thing in this kit is that it writes down why. The worst is that nothing enforces what it wrote down. CLAUDE.md declares four non-negotiables and the measurements say the kit breaks all four in its own source: 8 uses of the spacing scale, 31 colour literals across 7 files, 64 sub-44px targets, and a playground that demonstrates without documenting.

Biggest opportunity: the kit has no error state, no confirmation, and no toast, and its consumers restart containers and run SQL. Everything else is a sweep; that one is a hole.

# What's Working

1. The severity system is designed at three levels and holds at all three: Status.tsx:5-13 explains why --sev-* is separate from --stat-*; StatusDot ships a visually-hidden word; .ui-triage-row--critical:hover is written so the severity tint beats the hover tint.
2. The mobile work is real work, not a breakpoint: 100dvh assistant sheet with safe-area insets, .ui-log-body capped at 60vh with overscroll-behavior. Measured: at 390px scrollWidth === clientWidth, no page-level overflow; wide tables/editors correctly contained by their own overflow-x scrollers.
3. The reasoning is committed alongside the CSS, which is how a kit stays coherent across contributors.

# What the Kit Is Missing

Dialog/ConfirmDialog, Alert/Banner, Toast queue, ErrorState, field validation, Skeleton (shimmer keyframe exists, no component), Pagination, Breadcrumb, Menu/Dropdown, and — largest — layout primitives. No Stack, Cluster, Grid or Page shell; demo/demo.css invents .uidemo-grid and CLAUDE.md concedes apps use their own layout classes. Page composition is the thing most likely to make two apps look like different products.

Also: EmptyState has no action slot; Button.onClick is () => void with no event argument, so a button inside a clickable ListRow cannot stopPropagation; 10 of 11 motion declarations are unguarded by prefers-reduced-motion, including two infinite animations (ui-spin, ui-shimmer) — the one guard covers .ui-dot--pulse only.
